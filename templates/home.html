About CampusVote

CampusVote is a secure online voting platform designed specifically for academic institutions to conduct department-wise elections.

Our system ensures transparency, security, and convenience for both voters and administrators. With robust features and an intuitive interface, CampusVote makes the election process smooth and efficient.
