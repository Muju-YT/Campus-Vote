Voting Guidelines & Instructions

Please read the following guidelines carefully to ensure a smooth, secure, and compliant election process.

Strict Election Compliance Reminder

CampusVote enforces a strict **one-student, one-vote** policy. Any attempts to manipulate credentials, bypass session controls, or cast duplicate ballots are automatically logged in the system audit logs and will lead to immediate disciplinary proceedings by the college election committee.

How to Cast Your Ballot

1
Select an Election

Navigate to the **Cast Ballot** menu. Under the active elections list, select the specific department or general student election you wish to vote in.

2
Review Candidates

Examine candidate cards. Click **Read Manifesto** to evaluate each candidate's plan, experience, and academic goals before casting your vote.

3
Confirm & Submit

Click **Cast Vote** on your chosen candidate. A secure modal will display to confirm your selection. Verify details carefully and click submit.

4
Retain Your Receipt

Upon successful submission, the system generates a secure cryptographic transaction receipt. You can print or download this receipt under the **Vote Status** menu.
